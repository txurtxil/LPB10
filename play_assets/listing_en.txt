LMB10 is the unofficial app (not affiliated with Leapmotor) to get the
most out of your Leapmotor B10, C10 and family: real telemetry, costs and
savings, security and comfort.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Live dashboard: battery, real range, temperatures, vehicle state.
- Energy costs to the cent: AC/DC/HPC charges with your tariff (PVPC or
  custom), daily/monthly reports, PDF and an "efficiency receipt".
- Sentry mode: alerts if the car unlocks, opens, starts or moves, and arms
  the car's own sentry.
- Remote controls: climate, rapid heat/cool, lock, trunk, windows,
  sunshade, heated seats and steering wheel.
- Battery health: estimated usable capacity and passive drain.
- Trips with map and consumption, comparison vs a combustion car, home
  screen widget and electricity price to charge cheap.
- Android Auto: speedometer and car data on the car screen.
- Backups (Drive), MQTT/webhook telemetry and much more.

PRIVACY
No sign-up, no third-party analytics: your credentials stay on your phone
and the app talks directly to the Leapmotor cloud, like the official app.

REQUIRES
A Leapmotor account and a compatible vehicle. The app is independent: if
Leapmotor changes its API, some features may stop working until adapted.
